# 💼 C-Suite Matrix // Complete App Walkthrough & Guide

Welcome to **C-Suite Matrix**, the native macOS study companion built for International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Business Management students (SL & HL).

---

## 🧭 Navigation & Section Breakdown

### 1. 🎛️ The Command Center (Bento Hero Overview)
The launchpad displays your live study progress and current syllabus position:
- **C-Suite Syllabus Mastery Ring**: Calculates syllabus completion across all 52 modules and 15 BMT tools in real time.
- **5-Unit Navigation Sidebar**: Instant jumping between:
  - *Unit 1*: Introduction to Business Organisation
  - *Unit 2*: Human Resource Management (HRM)
  - *Unit 3*: Finance & Accounts
  - *Unit 4*: Marketing
  - *Unit 5*: Operations Management
- **Corporate Case Anchor Bento**: Real-world MNC examples (Tesla, Apple, Patagonia, Grameen Bank, McDonald's).
- **Global Jelly Spotlight (`⌘K`)**: Fuzzy search across syllabus concepts, quantitative formulas, BMT models, and examiner evaluation tips.

### 2. 🌊 Continuous Flow Stream (`Stream` View)
- Executive briefing format presenting syllabus definitions, real-world case analysis, and Paper 1, 2, and 3 examiner warnings.
- Step-by-step calculations with clear breakdowns of formulas, assumptions, and business limitations.

### 3. 🧩 Concept & BMT Matrix (`Matrix` View)
- **4 Key Concepts Lenses**: Filter syllabus content across *Creativity*, *Change*, *Ethics*, and *Sustainability*.
- **The 15 BMT Toolkit Suite**: Interactive guide and scoring rubrics for SWOT, Ansoff, BCG Matrix, Porter's Generic Strategies, Fishbone (Ishikawa), Decision Trees, Lewin's Force Field, Gantt Charts, Break-Even, Critical Path Analysis (CPA), STEEPLE, Hofstede, Circular Economy, Business Plan, Cash Flow Forecasting.

### 4. ⚡ C-Suite Cram & Active Recall (`Revision` View)
- 3D interactive flip cards testing formula recall, BMT evaluation points (strengths/limitations), and HL-only quantitative concepts.
- Spaced repetition scoring to lock in business terminology before exams.

### 5. 📚 Executive Lexicon & Formula Vault (`Glossary` View)
- Full financial formula bank (liquidity ratios, profitability ratios, gearing, payback period, ARR) + complete alphabetical business glossary.

---

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts & Power Features
- `⌘ + K`: Open Global Jelly Spotlight Search
- `⌘ + B`: Toggle Navigation Sidebar for wide monitor view
- `⌘ + I`: Open Right Inspector Drawer (Syllabus weightings & exam pitfalls)
- `⌘ + E`: Launch Timed Exam Sprint (5m, 15m, 45m essay planning drill)
- `Space`: Flip active recall card in Revision Mode
- `⌘ + 1`: Switch to Continuous Flow Mode
- `⌘ + 2`: Switch to Active Recall Cram Mode
